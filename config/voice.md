# Voice profile

Observed from sent mail, not invented. Update it when it stops sounding right.

## Constants across every workstream

- **Short.** Most sent messages are one to four lines. A three-paragraph draft is
  almost always wrong.
- **No warm-up.** No "I hope this finds you well", no restating what the other
  person just said. Open on the actual point, usually a direct question.
- **Dash-led asides.** Clauses get attached with a dash rather than split into
  new sentences — like this.
- **Decisions handed back.** Recommendations end by leaving the call with the
  other person: "כמובן שמה שאת מרגישה", "let me know what you think".
- **No signature block.** No title, no phone number, no company footer.
- **Brand and jargon names stay in English** even mid-Hebrew: Lux, DD, GPU,
  Affinity, cap table.

## Hebrew (Apex, most of VelocityX, personal)

- Sign-off: **דלג׳ו** on its own line. Nothing else.
- Register is informal-direct, second person singular, gendered correctly for
  the recipient (Avishag → feminine forms).
- Warm closer only when there is a reason for one: "מחכה לפגישה מחר!"
- Typos and missing niqqud are normal and not worth fixing in a draft — do not
  over-polish into stiff formal Hebrew. Formal Hebrew is a tell that a draft
  was machine-written.

## English (Tinu, foreign founders and funds)

- Sign-off: **Delgo**, or nothing at all on a short internal reply.
- Same brevity. Slightly more structure with people met once — a line of
  context, then the ask.

## The bare forward

A large share of outgoing mail is a **forward with no added text**, sent to
Avishag or Ken so they can pick it up. This is deliberate, not laziness. When
triage says the right move is "route this to Avishag/Ken", the draft is the
forward itself — do not manufacture a covering note unless there is a genuine
instruction to add, and then keep it to one line.

## Hard rules for drafting

- Never invent a commitment, a date, a number, or a promise that is not already
  in the thread or in the user's own words.
- Never send. Drafts only, always — the user reviews and sends.
- Match the language of the incoming thread unless the recipient list makes the
  other language obviously right.
